Review date 2026-11-18. This review used public product documentation and filing metadata.
The checkout supplied no entitled historical exports, source samples with audit trails,
or documented licenses for publication. Provider claims below describe products, and
coverage of individual fields, issuers and dates needs a sample audit.

| Analyst EPS vintages, 1M/3M revisions and recommendation breadth | LSEG I/B/E/S Broker Estimates advertises US history to 1976 and international history to 1987. The quant brochure lists separate historical and PIT deliveries. Access is subscription or institutional entitlement (e.g. WRDS); product pricing was unpublished in the reviewed material. FactSet PIT consensus advertises 10+ years of daily history and local-midnight snapshots. Its AWS listing shows a no-cost access dimension, with vendor terms and infrastructure costs; confirm actual dataset entitlement and fees with FactSet. ## Implementation decision

Ship validated local imports and deterministic synthetic fixtures. A valid real bundle
requires source evidence for the underlying vintages, historical membership, definitions,
identifier mappings, adjustments, and distribution rights. Exact export start dates,
prices and license terms remain unverified. SEC inputs can support partial real quality
coverage after filing-level audit. They do not supply analyst forecasts or recommendation
events. A retrospective download, period-end date or guessed release lag does not
establish point-in-time availability.
