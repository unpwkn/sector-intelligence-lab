Reviewed 18 November 2026 using provider documentation and public disclosures.
No source reviewed established a redistributable monthly panel of the 11 Sector SPDR ETFs and SPY with original vintages, positive-denominator checks and consistent coverage of the configured metrics. The shipped demonstration is synthetic. The importer accepts independently sourced ETF histories when those conditions can be substantiated for the series being researched.

## Research readiness

- Issuer observations are usable for source cross-checks. SEC filings and disclosed holdings support upstream diligence. Synthetic fixtures support offline testing. The import and scoring path is available for qualifying local exports.
- Real scoring requires a verified monthly series with enough valid points within 84 months, comparable portfolio definitions, reliable release and availability times, documented economics and licensed analysis rights. Forward estimates and specialty denominators are particular gaps.
- Before using a real series, reconcile selected monthly prints to original vintages, check historical asset and denominator coverage, and verify benchmarks use identical weighting rules, fiscal horizons, price bases and loss treatment. Historical ETF prices alone are insufficient. Assigning current holdings to past dates introduces survivorship and look-ahead bias.
